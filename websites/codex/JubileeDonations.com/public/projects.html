Overview

Each Jubilee project exists to fulfill a divine mission: to restore the voice of God’s Word and reach every heart on Earth.

These aren’t just innovations. They are living instruments of evangelism, discipleship, and awakening—built by faith, powered by AI, and grounded in the Holy Scriptures.

From restoring the original Bible to singing children’s songs that transform young hearts, our work is about more than technology. It’s about truth, presence, and power.

AI Bible Project

The First AI-Assisted Bible Translation & Restoration Initiative

The Jubilee Bible is the first translation in history built using AI trained on the original Hebrew, Greek, and Aramaic manuscripts.

Unlike traditional versions, Jubilee:

  • Restores sacred names, Hebraic idioms, and covenantal verb structures
  • Achieves 99% accuracy, compared to 70% in modern translations
  • Offers cultural and theological clarity based on the worldview of the early Church in the Book of Acts

This is not a paraphrase. This is a restoration.

Coming in 2025: translations into 100+ languages with contextual accuracy and immersive explanations.

RadioGPT

The Bible. On Air. In Every Tongue.

RadioGPT is the world’s first AI-powered radio evangelist, trained to broadcast Bible teaching, worship, storytelling, and prayer in over 100 languages.

What it does:

  • Hosts 24/7 spirit-led radio content across global networks
  • Engages listeners with interactive Bible Q&A and devotionals
  • Generates custom programming for local stations and unreached regions

With the voice of Jubilee, the gospel is no longer limited by time zones or translators.
