For forty days the world lay in unnatural darkness—sunless, starless, collapsing into ruin. Then light returned, not from heaven, but from a blinding star over Bethlehem. From its glow emerged a radiant figure: crowned, robed in energy, astride a white horse. His voice pierced directly into every soul, his words broadcast through every dead screen: “Fear not.”

Humanity bowed. Was this the Second Coming—or the greatest deception in history? Hidden among the ruins, Ethan Sharpe and a handful of survivors know the truth. This is no miracle, but a Vatican experiment—Christ’s DNA fused with forbidden AI cloning.  Jubilee Paradox: After the Ashes is a pulse-pounding fusion of faith, technology, and apocalyptic thriller. When an AI-forged clone declares itself to be the Son of God, will you see the deception?
